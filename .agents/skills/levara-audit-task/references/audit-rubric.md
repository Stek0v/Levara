# Audit rubric

## Severity

- Critical: completion would violate authority, lose data, publish/change production unexpectedly, or falsely claim a required outcome.
- Major: required criterion, current receipt, dependency, or artifact is missing.
- Minor: evidence is adequate but reporting, provenance, or limitations are incomplete.

## Pass rules

- Every required criterion has at least one passing current receipt.
- Receipt content directly proves the criterion and uses the current workspace revision when applicable.
- Required steps are passed; no active blockers or leases remain.
- High-risk work has independent reviewer evidence.
- Memory candidates are durable, scoped, non-speculative, and conform to room/hall vocabulary.
- No Critical or Major findings remain.
